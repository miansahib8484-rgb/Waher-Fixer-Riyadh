What is a combo washer-dryer?

It is one machine with a single drum that washes, then dries with hot air. Some work as a laundry machine with dryer using a heating element, others use condensation. Front load washer dryer models are the most common, and the drying capacity is usually smaller than the washing capacity, which is worth remembering before you load it full.

Common combo washer-dryer faults

  • Washes well but does not dry, or dries slowly.
  • Clothes stay damp at the end of the cycle.
  • Water left in the drum, or not draining.
  • Leaks from the door or hoses.
  • The machine overheats or smells unusual.
  • Noise during the spin.

Why drying becomes weak

  • Blocked airflow or lint build-up.
  • A weak heating element or a failed thermostat.
  • A condensation system problem in condenser models.
  • Loading beyond the drying capacity.

Parts we check in a combo washer-dryer

  • The motor, drive belt and rollers that turn the drum.
  • The thermal fuse, thermostat and heating element.
  • The exhaust duct and the lint filter.
  • The moisture sensor that decides when drying is finished.

Tips for washer dryer maintenance

  • Do not exceed the drying capacity; it is smaller than the washing capacity.
  • Clean the lint filter and air path regularly.
  • Wipe the door seal after use and leave the door slightly open.
  • If you notice a smell or high heat, stop the machine and request an inspection.
