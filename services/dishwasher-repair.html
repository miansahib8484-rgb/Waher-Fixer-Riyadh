Repair Guide & Common Faults

Dishwasher technician and service at your home

Need a dishwasher technician in Riyadh? We offer dishwasher repair and dishwasher service at your home, from a quick fix when the machine won't drain to regular dishwasher maintenance. We explain the fault and the cost before any work begins, with a 30-day warranty on parts and labor.

Common dishwasher faults

The most common complaints are poor cleaning with food left on dishes, water that won't drain, leaks, and a dishwasher that doesn't heat or won't start a cycle. These usually trace back to a blocked bottom filter or spray arms, a failed drain pump, or a fault in the heating element or door seal. We inspect the unit first and then identify the faulty part.

Why dishes stay dirty or look cloudy

White deposits on dishes and glassware are usually caused by hard water and call for dishwasher salt and a suitable rinse aid. Leftover food points to blocked spray arms or filter, or to dishes loaded in a way that blocks the water. Clean the filter and spray arms first; if the problem continues, the machine needs an inspection.

Bad smells and error codes

A bad smell usually means residue stuck in the filter or drain hose. You can run a hot cleaning cycle with a dishwasher cleaner. Error codes differ by brand; the appliance manual explains them and the technician confirms them by inspection.

Regular dishwasher maintenance

Scrape large food scraps before loading, clean the filter regularly, check the spray arms for blockages, don't overload the racks, and use salt and rinse aid as the manufacturer recommends. Don't use hand dishwashing liquid in the machine, as it causes excess foam and can lead to leaks.

How a dishwasher works and which parts fail

Water enters the dishwasher through the inlet valve, the heating element warms it, the circulation pump drives it through the spray arms onto the dishes, the filter traps food residue, and the drain pump removes the dirty water. The detergent dispenser and rinse aid release at the right moment, the door seal prevents leaks, and the control board runs the cycle. That is why the common faults come from a blocked filter or spray arms, a worn drain pump or heating element, a damaged door seal, or a faulty detergent dispenser.

What to prepare before the technician arrives

Note the brand and model of the dishwasher (on a label inside the door frame or on its side), photograph any code on the display, empty the racks if you can, and tell us whether the problem is draining, cleaning, heating or leaking. If you use dishwasher salt and rinse aid, mention it.
