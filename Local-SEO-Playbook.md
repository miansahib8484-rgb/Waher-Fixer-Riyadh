# Local SEO Playbook: Automatic Washing Machine Repair, Riyadh

Primary service: automatic washing machine repair. Secondary: dishwasher repair, dryer repair. Base: Al Yarmuk, Riyadh.
Status: the on-site parts of this playbook are already in the build (areas hub rewritten, local FAQs, emergency block, call CTAs, areaServed schema). Everything below that says "GBP" or "off-site" must be done by the owner.

## 1. Local hub page (already built): /areas/east-north-and-south-riyadh and /ar/areas/...
- SEO title (kept, 50 chars): Washing Machine Repair East, North & South Riyadh
- Alternative title to test (59 chars): Washing Machine Repair in Riyadh Neighborhoods | Home Visits
- Meta description (156 chars): Washing machine repair in Al Yarmuk, Al Malaz, Hittin, Al Malqa, Al Shifa and across Riyadh. Home visits, cost explained first, 30-day warranty. Call now.
- URL slug: keep /areas/east-north-and-south-riyadh (already indexed-ready; do not change after launch).
- Sections: area by area (East, Central, North, South), what people say on the phone, live-leak emergency steps, call CTA strip, 8 local FAQs.

## 2. Local schema (already in the hub page, plus suggestions)
Done: FAQPage with the 8 visible FAQs; LocalBusiness `areaServed` = Riyadh + 27 neighborhoods; `knowsAbout`; `serviceType` on each service offer.
Suggested next:
- `hasMap` on LocalBusiness pointing to your real Google Maps place URL (needs the real URL).
- `sameAs` with the Google Business Profile, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn URLs that really exist.
- `openingHoursSpecification` currently says open 24 hours, every day. Confirm this is true, or change it to your real hours.
- `aggregateRating` / `review` ONLY from real Google reviews shown on the page. Do not add fake ones.
- `geo` is already present (Al Yarmuk coordinates). Double-check the pin matches your real Google Maps location.

## 3. Google Business Profile ideas
- Primary category: Washing machine repair service. Secondary: Dishwasher repair service, Appliance repair service (a GBP category is allowed to be broader than the website headings).
- Business name: use your real, registered name only. Do not add keywords to the name.
- Service areas: add the neighborhoods listed on the hub page (Al Yarmuk, Al Naseem, Al Rawdah, Al Malaz, Al Olaya, Al Wurud, An Nakheel, Hittin, Al Malqa, Al Sahafah, Al Shifa and the rest). Keep the list to areas you really visit.
- Services list: Automatic washing machine repair, Front-load washer repair, Top-load washer repair, Dishwasher repair, Dryer repair, Washer-dryer repair. Add a one-line description for each, no prices unless real.
- Description (750 characters max): lead with automatic washing machine repair in Riyadh, home visits, cost explained before work, 30-day warranty, based in Al Yarmuk.
- Photos: real workshop and job photos with the machine brand visible; add 3 to 5 new photos a month.
- Posts (weekly): one fault per post (not draining, not spinning, leaking) with a call button. Reuse the symptom guides.
- Q&A: seed the 5 shortest FAQs from the hub page.
- Reviews: ask every customer after the repair; reply to every review by mentioning the fault and the neighborhood in natural words. Do not buy or fake reviews.
- Messaging: turn on WhatsApp and call buttons; add the same phone number used on the site.

## 7. Call-focused CTA placement (already in the build)
- Hero and sticky bar on every page (call + WhatsApp)
- Mid-page CTA strip on the areas hub, directly after the live-leak steps
- End-of-page CTA block per page type (service, problem, brand, blog, area)
- Sidebar sticky card on desktop

## 8. What I did not do on purpose
- No fake reviews or review-style quotes. The "what people say on the phone" list is typical fault wording, not testimonials.
- No new coverage claims beyond the neighborhoods you already listed.
- No landmark claims you cannot verify.
