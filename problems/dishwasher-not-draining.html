Dishwasher Not Draining: Common Causes and Fixes

You open the dishwasher expecting clean, dry plates and find a pool of grey water at the bottom. Standing water is usually a blockage, not a dead machine, and often something you can clear yourself in a few minutes.

Why a dishwasher stops draining

  • Filter blocked with food residue, labels or broken glass.
  • Drain hose kinked or blocked.
  • Drain pump worn out or its impeller jammed.
  • Blocked connection to the kitchen sink trap or the household drain line.
  • Control board fault (rare).

Check safely first

  • Switch the dishwasher off at the power.
  • Remove the bottom rack, then the filter, and rinse it under running water.
  • Look around the sump for trapped objects such as glass fragments or fruit stones, and handle them carefully.
  • Check the drain hose behind the machine for kinks.
  • Run a short cycle to see whether the water drains.

Keep your hand away from the pump. If these steps don't work, call the technician.

Error codes and signs

On many LG dishwashers the code OE points to a drainage problem, and on many Samsung models it is 5E or 5C. Codes vary by brand and model, so check the manual and tell us the code when you call.

How the technician diagnoses it

  • Inspect the filter and the sump.
  • Test the drain pump and confirm its impeller turns.
  • Check the drain hose, the sink connection and any non-return valve.
  • Check the control board output, then run a full cycle after the repair.
