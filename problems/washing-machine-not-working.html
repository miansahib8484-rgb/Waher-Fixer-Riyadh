Washing Machine Not Working: What to Check First

When a washing machine won't start or stops suddenly, the cause is sometimes simple and needs no technician. We begin with quick checks, then the faults that need a specialist.

Quick checks before you call

  • Check that power is reaching it: try another socket and check the breaker.
  • Make sure the door is fully closed and nothing is caught in it.
  • Open the water tap; some machines will not start without water.
  • Check the child lock: on some LG machines the code CL means it is on, not that something is broken.
  • Unplug the machine for a few minutes, then switch it on again.

Possible faults

  • Faulty door lock or lid switch.
  • Water supply problem (inlet valve or its filter).
  • Control panel or button fault.
  • Problem with the power button or cable.
  • Sensor or heating fault that stops the program.

The machine stops mid-cycle

This can happen because water is not draining, the load is unbalanced, not enough water is coming in, or an overheating protection has triggered. Check the code on the display if there is one, and tell the technician.

Washing machine door lock repair

When the door lock or lid switch is the cause, washing machine door lock repair usually means replacing the faulty part. Do not force the lock open, as you can damage the door or the machine; call us and we will check it at your home.

How we check a machine that will not work

  • Confirm power reaches the machine: the breaker, the socket and the cable.
  • Check the door lock and the lid switch.
  • Check the water inlet valve and its filter.
  • Check the control panel, the fuse and the buttons, and cancel the child lock if it is on.

When to call us

If the machine still does not work after these checks, or you smell burning, or see sparks or hear crackling, unplug it immediately and call us. We visit you in Riyadh and explain the fault and the cost before starting, with a 30-day warranty on parts and labor.
