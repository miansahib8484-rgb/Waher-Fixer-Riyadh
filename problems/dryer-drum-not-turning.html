Dryer Drum Not Turning or Making Noise: Causes and Fixes

If you hear the motor but the drum does not turn, or the dryer clicks, squeals or thumps, the cause is usually the belt, the rollers or the motor. Here are the causes and what to do safely.

Why a dryer drum stops turning

  • The drum belt is broken or loose.
  • The idler pulley or the drum rollers are worn.
  • A fault in the motor or its start capacitor.
  • The door switch does not confirm the door is closed, so the cycle will not start.
  • An object trapped between the drum and the cabinet.
  • Worn drum glides or bearings.

What each noise usually means

  • A sharp squeal: usually the idler pulley or worn rollers.
  • A repeated thump or rumble each turn: usually worn drum glides or a damaged roller.
  • Clicking: a small object trapped, or buttons and clips in the load.
  • A hum with no turning: usually the belt or the motor.

Check safely first

  • Unplug the dryer.
  • Remove the clothes and check that nothing is trapped.
  • Turn the drum by hand: if it spins with no resistance at all, the belt may be broken.
  • Make sure the door closes fully.

Do not open the dryer's cabinet yourself; it contains electrical and heating parts.

How the technician diagnoses it

  • Inspect the belt, the idler pulley and the drum rollers.
  • Test the motor and the start capacitor.
  • Check the door switch, the drum glides and the bearings.
  • Run a full cycle after the repair.
