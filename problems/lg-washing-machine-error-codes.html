LG Washing Machine Error Codes: What Each One Means

Not every code on an LG display is a breakdown. Some are normal messages, and some you can clear in a minute. This page sorts them, so you know when to relax and when to call.

Is it really a fault? CL, PF and UE first

Three codes are often not a breakdown at all. CL means the child lock is on. The machine is working as designed, and your manual shows how to switch the lock off. PF means power failed during a cycle. Press start again and it usually carries on.

UE means the load is unbalanced. Open the door, spread the clothes out and avoid washing one heavy item alone. Make sure the machine stands level. Only if it keeps coming back with a normal load do you need to look further.

LG error codes in a table

Meanings can differ by model, and some machines use extra variants. Check the manual for your exact machine. The codes below are the widely documented ones.

Door codes: dE, dE1, dE2 and dE4

The machine will not start a wash unless it senses that the door is shut and locked. A sleeve or sock caught in the rubber seal is the most common cause, and a firm close often solves it.

The different numbers can point to different parts of the door circuit, and the exact meaning depends on the model. If the code stays after you check the seal, do not force the door or try to bypass the lock. If a full drum is stuck shut, our guide on a door that will not open explains what is safe to try.

Water codes: IE, OE, FE and PE

IE says too little water arrived. Check the tap and hose first. OE says water did not leave, so look at the drain hose and the pump filter, as in our page on a washing machine not draining.

FE and PE are different. FE points to too much water, and PE to the sensor that reads the water level. Neither has a safe home repair. Switch off, close the tap, and restart once. If you see FE again, leave the machine off and tell us.

  • Unplug and close the tap before touching any hose.
  • Keep a towel and a shallow tray ready, because some water will come out of the filter.
  • Never open anything inside the machine.

Motor and heater codes: LE and tE

LE on many LG models relates to the motor, which turns the drum. A heavy wet load or a stuck item can trigger it once. If it returns with a small load, the motor or its control may need testing.

tE points to the heater or the temperature sensor. A simple clue helps the technician: does the machine still wash with cold water? Please tell us.

A short checklist before you call

  • Write down the code and when it appears: filling, washing, draining or spinning.
  • Find the model number on the label, usually inside the door frame or on the back.
  • Try one restart after waiting a few minutes. Do not keep repeating it.
  • Note any water on the floor, burning smell or loud noise.
