Dishwasher Not Cleaning Dishes Properly: Causes and Fixes

Dishes that come out gritty, cloudy or with food still stuck usually point to a few specific causes: blocked spray arms, a dirty filter, hard water, overloaded racks or the wrong detergent. Here is how to tell them apart.

Why a dishwasher stops cleaning well

  • Spray-arm holes blocked with scale or food.
  • A dirty filter returning dirt to the dishes.
  • Racks overloaded or dishes blocking the water.
  • Too little salt or rinse aid, or the wrong detergent or amount.
  • A weak circulation pump or heating element.
  • A detergent dispenser that does not open at the right time.

Check safely first

  • Switch off the power, take out the spray arms and clear their holes with a thin wooden pick.
  • Rinse the filter under running water.
  • Make sure no dish stops the arms from turning.
  • Use detergent, salt and rinse aid as the manufacturer recommends.
  • Run an empty hot cycle with a dishwasher cleaner.

Cloudy glass and a white film

A white or cloudy film on glassware is usually caused by hard water and calls for dishwasher salt and a suitable rinse aid. Food left on dishes points to blocked spray arms or filter, or to weak water circulation.

How the technician diagnoses it

  • Inspect the spray arms and the filter.
  • Check the incoming water pressure, the inlet valve and its filter.
  • Test the circulation pump and the heating element.
  • Check the detergent and rinse aid dispenser.
