Dishwasher Leaking Water: Find the Source and Fix It

Water on the kitchen floor in front of the dishwasher is stressful, especially near cabinets. The good news: it is usually one of four things, the door seal, a hose, the wrong soap, or a valve, and each leaves a different pattern.

What to do as soon as you notice a leak

  • Stop the dishwasher and unplug it.
  • Turn off the water tap feeding it.
  • Dry the area and remove the pooled water.
  • Work out where the water is coming from before you call.

Where the leak comes from and the likely cause

  • From the door: the door seal is worn or dirty, or the door is not closing properly.
  • From the bottom at the front: excess foam from using hand dishwashing liquid.
  • From the back: the inlet or drain hose or its connection.
  • From underneath, with the machine not filling: the inlet valve or the float safety switch.
  • From the sump during the cycle: a cracked tub or a worn pump seal.

Too much foam

Hand dishwashing liquid makes a huge amount of foam inside a dishwasher, and it escapes around the door and looks like a leak. Use a detergent made for dishwashers, in the recommended amount.

How we find the source of a leak

  • Dry the sump and run a short cycle to watch where the water appears.
  • Inspect the door seal, hinges and door latch.
  • Check the inlet and drain hoses and their connections.
  • Test the inlet valve, the float safety switch and the pump seal.
