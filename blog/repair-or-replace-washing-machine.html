Repair or Replace a Washing Machine: How to Decide

The repair-or-replace decision depends on three things: the type of fault, the age of the machine, and the repair cost compared with the price of a similar new machine.

Published by Automatic Washing Machine Repair · Last updated: October 2026

This page is part of our guide to automatic washing machine repair in Riyadh.

What affects the decision

  • The age and general condition of the machine.
  • Whether the fault is a single part or several.
  • How many times it has been repaired before.
  • The repair cost compared with a similar new machine.
  • Whether the part is available.

Faults that are usually worth repairing

FaultUsually worth repairing?
Blocked filter or hoseYes, low cost
Drain pumpUsually yes
Door lock or lid switchUsually yes
Drive belt or carbon brushesUsually yes
Control board on an older machineDepends on age and cost
Worn bearings or a cracked tub on an old machineReplacing is often better value

A simple rule of thumb

If the repair cost approaches half the price of a similar new machine, consider replacing it. You will find more detail in our washing machine repair cost guide.

Before you decide

  • Ask for a clear diagnosis of the fault.
  • Ask for the parts and labor costs itemized.
  • Ask how long the warranty is and what it covers.
