LG vs Samsung Washing Machine: Common Issues Compared

LG or Samsung? In Riyadh homes you will find both everywhere, and when they break, the faults are surprisingly similar. The main difference is what the display tells you. Here is a side-by-side.

Error codes side by side

Codes vary by model, so treat this table as a guide:

Drainage problemOE5E or SE (5C on some models)

Door lock problemdEDE or dE

Suspected leak—LE or LC
Water level sensorPEVaries by model

Faults shared by both brands

  • A blocked drain pump filter or a worn drain pump.
  • The door lock.
  • The water inlet valve and its filter.
  • The drive belt and the motor.
  • Shock absorbers and bearings.
  • The control board.

What really changes the repair

The type of machine (front-load or top-load) and its age usually matter more than the brand. Read our guide to front-load vs top-load faults, or see our Samsung washing machine repair and LG washing machine repair pages.

What to tell the technician

  • The brand and model of the machine.
  • The code on the display.
  • A description of the fault and when it started.

Which brand is easier to repair?

We do not rank brands. We look at each machine, and the bill depends on the part, the model and the age. What we do promise is to explain the fault and the cost before any work begins, with a 30-day warranty on parts and labor.
