Which east Riyadh districts do you visit?

Our technicians visit homes in Al Yarmuk, Al Naseem, Al Rawdah, Al Hamra, Granada, Al Khaleej, Al Qadisiyah and Al Rimal. If you live in Al Yarmuk or Al Qadisiyah, those districts have their own short pages.

Because we are based in the east, you are close to our base. Even so, the visit time depends on the day and the hour you call, so we confirm it with you instead of promising a number in advance.

How do I describe my address on the phone?

A clear address saves a second call. Say it in this order, and the technician arrives knowing where to go.

  • The district, for example Al Hamra or Granada.
  • The street, or a place nearby that you are happy to name.
  • Villa or apartment building, and the floor if it is an apartment.
  • Where the machine stands: kitchen, service room or balcony.
  • A phone number that someone will answer when the technician is outside.

What if I cannot explain the way?

Send a location pin on WhatsApp. It is usually clearer than words. If the entrance is hard to see from the road, tell us which gate or door to use.

What does a visit include?

The technician comes to your home and looks at the machine where it stands. The visit follows the same order every time. Most repairs are done the same day you contact us, though it depends on the fault and on whether a part is needed. The full service is described on the washing machine repair page.

  • Diagnosis first, before anything is taken apart.
  • The fault and the cost are explained to you before any work.
  • Nothing starts until you agree.
  • A 30-day warranty on parts and labor for the repair.

Water on the floor right now?

Do these in order. Our leaking guide explains the likely causes afterwards.

  • Close the water tap behind the machine.
  • Unplug the machine. If the floor near the socket is wet, switch off the breaker at the board and do not touch the plug.
  • Lay towels so the water does not reach other rooms.
  • Photograph the leak and the display, then send the photos on WhatsApp.
  • Call and say the leak is still running, so we can look for the earliest time.

Does it matter if I live in a villa or an apartment?

It does not stop the repair. It only changes what we ask you. For an apartment, tell us the floor, whether there is a lift and whether the building has rules about visits. For a villa, tell us whether the machine is inside the house or in a separate room. Our east, north and south Riyadh guide shows how the three regions compare.

What should I have ready?

Customers rate us 4.8 on Google from 264 reviews, and the messages that help us most are short and specific. Have these ready.

  • The brand and model, usually on a sticker on the machine.
  • Any code on the display. Its meaning depends on the model, so check your manual and send us the code.
  • When the fault started and what the machine was doing at the time.

What if the machine needs a part?

Some faults need a part. The technician tells you so at the diagnosis, together with the cost, and you decide. If the part is not with him, we explain the next step. Most repairs are done the same day, but a missing part can change that, so ask us at the start.

Is my old machine worth repairing?

Ask the technician. You hear the fault and the cost first, so the choice stays with you. Our repair or replace guide lists questions to ask yourself before you decide.

Do you repair other appliances in the east?

Yes. The same visit works for dishwashers, dryers and washer-dryer combos. See the pages for dishwasher repair and dryer repair.
