Does summer heat and dust matter for my machine?

Many homes keep the washing machine on a balcony or in an open service area. Strong heat and dust can be hard on a machine kept there. Dust may collect around the vents, the detergent drawer and the door seal. Sun can also age plastic and rubber parts faster. None of this is a fault by itself, and it depends on the model and where it stands. It is still worth a little care.

Simple care you can do yourself

Always unplug the machine and close the tap first. Then:

  • Wipe the door seal and the detergent drawer area with a damp cloth.
  • Leave the door ajar after the last wash so the drum can dry.
  • Keep the machine out of direct sun when you can.
  • Check the hoses for cracks or stiff, dried rubber, and tell us if you see any.

What should I tell you about the fault?

A short and clear description lets the technician bring the right tools.

  • The brand and model, and whether it is front-load or top-load.
  • What happened and when: during the wash, the rinse or the spin.
  • Any code on the display, with a photo. Check your manual for its meaning.
  • Whether the machine stands indoors, on a balcony or in a service room.

Which faults are most common?

Water that will not drain, a drum that will not spin and leaks are the three we hear most. Each has a guide: not spinning and not draining.

Water on the floor right now?

Close the tap behind the machine and unplug it. If the floor near the socket is wet, switch off the breaker instead of touching the plug. Put towels down, photograph the leak and send it to us. Our leaking guide explains the usual causes afterwards.

Should I move things before the technician comes?

A clear path helps. Move baskets, detergent bottles and mats away from the machine. If it stands on a balcony, tell us about steps or narrow doors. Do not drag a full machine yourself.

Can I take out the wet clothes?

If the door opens normally, yes. Take the clothes out before we come. If the door is locked and water is inside, leave it and tell us. Never force the door.

What if I cannot find the model number?

It is not a problem. Send a photo of the front of the machine and tell us the brand. If you can read any label on the sides or the back, send that too. We will work from what you have and ask for more only if needed.
