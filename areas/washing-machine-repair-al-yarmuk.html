How does a washing machine repair visit in Al Yarmuk work?

Being based in Al Yarmuk means your request starts from the same neighbourhood as our workshop. The steps are simple, and they are the same for every home we visit across Riyadh. Our customers rate us 4.8 on Google from 264 reviews, and most of that comes from keeping the process clear.

  • You message or call us and describe the problem in a few words.
  • We agree a visit. Most repairs are done the same day you contact us, but it depends on the fault and the parts needed.
  • The technician checks the machine at your home and tells you the fault.
  • You hear the cost before any work begins. Nothing starts until you agree.
  • If you agree, the repair is done and tested. Parts and labor carry a 30-day warranty.

How do I send my location as a WhatsApp pin?

A pin is the easiest way to show us exactly where the machine is. It saves a long explanation of the way to your door. The menu names differ a little between phones, but it usually goes like this.

  • Open a chat with us on WhatsApp.
  • Tap the paperclip icon or the plus sign next to the message box.
  • Choose Location.
  • Tap Send your current location if you are at home. If you are somewhere else, move the map to your house and pick that spot.
  • Add a short line: the floor, the flat number and the name on the door or bell.

What should I write in my first message?

Four short details help us plan the visit properly. You do not need technical words. Plain description is enough.

  • The brand and the model, if you can read it from the label.
  • What happens: no water, no spinning, a leak, a noise, or a message on the screen.
  • Whether the machine is front-load or top-load.
  • Your location pin.

What should be ready at home when the technician arrives?

A few small things save the first ten minutes of the visit.

  • A clear path from the front door to the machine.
  • A reachable power socket and a tap that turns easily.
  • The load taken out of the drum, unless the door is locked.
  • An adult at home who can agree to the repair.

What if my machine is still under the brand's own warranty?

Read the warranty terms first, or ask the brand. We are an independent repair service, not the official service center of any brand, and work by an independent repairer can affect some brand warranties. If the machine is out of warranty, or you simply want an answer quickly, we are glad to look at it.

What happens at the end of the visit?

After the repair, the technician tests the machine with you watching, so you can see the fault is gone. Ask any question you have about how to use or care for it. Keep the date of the visit, because the 30-day warranty on parts and labor starts from the repair.
