Washing machine repair across Riyadh, area by area

Riyadh is a big city, and a repair visit looks a little different depending on where you live. We are based in Al Yarmuk, in the east, and technicians head out from there to the neighborhoods below. Tell us yours when you call and we will confirm the visit time, because nobody wants a promise that ignores the traffic on King Fahd Road at five in the evening.

Washing machine repair in East Riyadh

We serve East Riyadh neighborhoods, including:

  • Al Yarmuk and Al Naseem
  • Al Rawdah and Al Hamra
  • Granada and Al Khaleej
  • Al Qadisiyah and Al Rimal

East Riyadh is home base, so Al Yarmuk, Al Naseem and Al Rawdah are the neighborhoods closest to us on the map. Plenty of homes here are villas with the washing machine in a service room at the back, which makes life easy for a technician: space to pull the machine out and a floor drain nearby. Apartment blocks need a little more planning, mostly around the drain stack and where the machine sits.

Looking for washing machine repair in Al Yarmuk? Call us with your neighborhood and the fault, and we will confirm the visit time.

Washing machine repair in central Riyadh

Al Olaya, Al Malaz, Al Wurud and An Nakheel sit toward the middle of the city, and the Olaya side is easy to place: it is the area around the Kingdom Centre. Many homes here are apartments in mid-rise buildings, so it helps if you tell us the floor, where the machine is plumbed in, any building rules on visiting hours for technicians, and where we can park.

Washing machine repair in North Riyadh

  • Al Malqa and Al Arid
  • Al Narjis and Al Yasmin

Hittin, Al Malqa, Al Sahafah and the neighborhoods near the Northern Ring Road are mostly newer residential areas. In compounds, a technician's name may need to be on the gate list, so send us the compound name and a contact number when you book.

Looking for washing machine repair in North Riyadh? It is the same service: call us and tell us your neighborhood.

Washing machine repair in South Riyadh

We serve South Riyadh neighborhoods, including:

  • Al Shifa and Al Aziziyah

Al Shifa, Al Aziziyah, Al Suwaidi and the south of the city are further from our base in the east, so the visit time depends more on the hour you call. Give us your neighborhood and we will be straight with you about when we can get there.

How a home visit works

  • You describe the fault or send a photo of the code on WhatsApp.
  • The technician comes to your home and inspects the machine where it stands.
  • We explain the fault and the cost before any work begins.
  • We repair the machine with a 30-day warranty on parts and labor.

What to prepare before the visit

  • The brand and model of the machine.
  • The code on the display, if there is one.
  • Your neighborhood and a description of the fault and when it started.

What people usually tell us on the phone

These are the descriptions we hear most often, and each one points to the right guide:

  • “It fills with water but never drains”
  • “It shakes and bangs on the spin cycle”
  • “There is water under the door or behind the machine”
  • “The drum won't turn”
  • “There is a code on the display” (LG)
  • “There is a code on the display” (Samsung)
  • “It won't turn on at all”

Water on the floor right now?

If the machine is leaking this minute, here is what helps before the technician arrives:

  • Turn off the water tap behind the machine.
  • Unplug the machine. If the floor around the socket is wet, switch off the breaker at the board instead of touching the plug.
  • Put towels down so the water does not reach other rooms.
  • Photograph the leak and the display, and send the photos on WhatsApp.
  • Call us and say it is a live leak, so we can give you the earliest time available.
