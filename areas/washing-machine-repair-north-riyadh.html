First, a safety rule

Unplug the machine and close the water tap before you touch anything. Do not open electrical parts. Do not force a locked door.

The five calls we hear most

Find your fault in the table and try only the safe first check. If it does not help, stop and call.

Why will my machine not start?

Start with the simple things. Is the socket working, is the plug in firmly, is the tap open and is the door fully closed? Many machines refuse to start if the door is not registering as shut. If all of that is fine and the display stays dark, the cause may be inside, and it is a job for a technician. Our page on a machine that is not working lists more checks.

What should I photograph before I call?

Photos replace a long phone call. Take these, and send them on WhatsApp.

  • The display, if it shows a code or a light.
  • The model sticker, usually on the door frame or the back.
  • The wet spot on the floor, with the machine in the frame.
  • A short video of the sound, if the noise is the problem.

Compounds and gated buildings

Many homes in the north are in compounds. A technician may need to be registered at the gate. Send us the compound name and a contact number when you book, and tell us the villa or apartment number. This saves time at the entrance. The same goes for Al Yasmin and the other districts above.

Which of these cannot wait?

A leak that is spreading, or a locked door with water still inside, needs attention first. A machine that spins slowly can usually wait until a time suits you. Use your judgment: if water is moving across the floor, treat it as urgent and tell us so when you call.

What can I send on WhatsApp?

One message is enough. For example: your district, villa or apartment, the brand, the fault in one sentence, and the photos from the list above. The technician reads it before the visit and comes prepared.

How does the visit work?

The technician diagnoses the machine where it stands, then tells you the fault and the cost. Nothing starts until you agree. Most repairs are done the same day you contact us, depending on the fault and the parts. Parts and labor carry a 30-day warranty.
