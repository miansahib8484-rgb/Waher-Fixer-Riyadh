Most common Samsung washing machine faults

  • Not draining, with water left in the drum.
  • Not spinning, or shaking because of an unbalanced load.
  • Water leaking from the door, hoses or detergent drawer.
  • Machine not filling with water.
  • Door lock problems.
  • Noise during the spin.

Samsung washing machine error codes

Codes vary by model; these are common meanings we see on many models:

  • 5E or SE: drainage problem.
  • 4E or 4C: water inlet problem.
  • UE: unbalanced load.
  • DE or dE: door lock problem.
  • LE or LC: suspected water leak.
  • 3E: motor or motor sensor problem.

These codes are a guide only; check your manual and tell us the code and model when you call.

What we need from you when you call

  • The machine model, usually on a label at the back or near the door.
  • The code shown on the display, if any.
  • A description of the fault and when it started.

How we diagnose a Samsung washing machine

We start by reading the code on the display, then check the part linked to it: the drain filter and pump for 5E or SE, the inlet valve and its filter for 4E, the door lock for DE, the water level sensor, and the motor and its sensor for 3E, before we suggest replacing anything.
