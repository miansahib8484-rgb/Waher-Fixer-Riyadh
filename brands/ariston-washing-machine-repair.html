What goes wrong with Ariston washers

Ariston machines are common in Riyadh homes, and some have been running for many years. Faults depend on the model and its age, so the list below is a guide. The real cause is confirmed only when we look at the machine.

  • Drain pump trouble. The machine fills but leaves water behind, or hums without emptying.
  • A door lock that will not engage, so the cycle never starts.
  • A weak inlet valve. The drum fills slowly, or overfills.
  • On older machines with a brush motor, a drive belt or carbon brushes that have worn out. Symptoms are a slipping drum, a burning smell or a motor that hums and does not turn.
  • A control board fault. The machine shows odd lights, stops mid-cycle or does not respond to the buttons.

Old machine or new one: does it matter?

A little. Older Ariston washers often use a belt and brushes, which are normal wear parts. Newer machines tend to use a different motor with fewer wear parts, but they rely more on the control board. We check the machine in front of us, not the label on the brochure.

If the machine is very old and the fault is large, we will tell you. Our guide on signs a washer needs repair lists what to watch for.

Things you can check first

Always switch off, unplug the machine and close the water tap before you touch anything.

  • Clean the pump filter if your model has a small hatch at the lower front. Use a towel and a tray.
  • Look at the drain hose for kinks.
  • Check the door seal for a trapped sleeve.
  • Reduce the load if the drum does not spin.

A leak or a drum that will not spin

Water on the floor can come from a loose hose, a worn door seal or a pump. Read our page on a leaking washing machine for the safe checks. A drum that does not turn is covered in washing machine not spinning.

Please do not open electrical parts and do not bypass the door lock.
